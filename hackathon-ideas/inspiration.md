# Your Inspiration Bookmarks

This is Parth's Rule 2 made into a file. Whenever you see a cool demo, launch, art piece, game, or weird project, paste its link here with a few words about why it caught your eye. You don't need to think about building it. The skill mixes these into ideas first, before anything it finds on the web.

Format: `- YYYY-MM-DD | link | why it caught my eye`
