# Remix Bank - Forgotten and Nostalgic Formats

Seeds for Rule 2's "a very recent idea + a forgotten one". These are starting points written for this skill, not taken from Parth's article. Always prefer a format tied to the event's city, culture, or audience. Pick one, then ask: "What if the sponsor's new capability powered THIS?"

## Games and toys
SimCity / city builders · Tamagotchi / virtual pets · choose-your-own-adventure books · text adventures (Zork) · point-and-click mysteries · Pokemon-style collecting · Snakes and Ladders / Ludo · Carrom · board-game RPGs · flipbooks · pop-up books · View-Master · Etch A Sketch · jigsaw puzzles · crossword and word-search · trading cards · pinball · arcade high-score tables · Tetris · kaleidoscopes · paper dolls

## Media and interfaces
Radio shows and radio dramas · walkie-talkies · pagers · answering machines · telegrams · postcards · pen pals · newspapers and comic strips · Sunday supplements · mixtapes · jukeboxes · slide projectors · filmstrips · VHS rental stores · Ceefax/teletext · early web (GeoCities, guestbooks, webrings) · MSN/Orkut-style status · Winamp skins · Clippy-style helpers · screensavers · desktop pets · CRT terminals · DOS prompts

## Local and cultural (India examples; swap for the event's region)
Doordarshan-era TV shows · Amar Chitra Katha comics · Panchatantra tales · street vendors, autos, and chai stalls · railway enquiry announcements · STD/PCO booths · kirana stores and khata ledgers · puppet shows (kathputli) · folk art (Madhubani, Warli, Gond) · harikatha / oral storytelling · cricket commentary on radio · wedding invitations · festival rangoli · Bollywood film posters and trailers

## Physical-world metaphors for invisible things
A codebase as a city · data as weather · agents as a construction crew · a queue as a railway station · logs as a newspaper · a model's thinking as a theatre stage · a portfolio as a garden · a conversation as a walk through a market

## How to use
1. Pick 2-3 seeds that match the sponsor's strength (visual model -> visual format; voice model -> radio, phone, market chatter; agents -> crews, cities, stations).
2. Collide each with a fresh spark from Step 2.
3. Keep only collisions where the old format makes the new tech **easier to see in 60 seconds**.
